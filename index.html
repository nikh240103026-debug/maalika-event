The Studio

We transform ordinary venues into unforgettable celebrations.

Maalika Event is a flower and event decoration studio in Patna. We design and build the spaces where celebrations happen — from intimate family functions to grand wedding receptions — with fresh florals, considered styling and a team that takes care of every detail, so you don't have to.

  • Custom Floral Designs

    Arrangements composed around your colours, theme and venue — never copied from a catalogue.

  • Wedding & Event Decoration

    Mandaps, stages, entrances and receptions, styled as one cohesive story.

  • Premium Venue Styling

    Drapery, lighting and florals that change how a room feels the moment guests arrive.

  • Attention to Every Detail

    From the welcome board to the last centrepiece, nothing is an afterthought.
